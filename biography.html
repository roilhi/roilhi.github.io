Short bio

Roilhi Frajo Ibarra-Hernández received the BEng degree in Electronics and Telecommunications from Universidad Autónoma de Zacatecas (UAZ) in 2012, the MSc in 2015 and in 2019 the PhD degree in Telecommunications from the Center of Scientific Research and Higher Education of Ensenada (CICESE). During his doctoral studies, he made a research stay at the INRIA Research Center in Rennes, France joining the PANAMA team as former member. Here, the research conducted was in signal processing methods for the automatic classification of audio signals based on sparse representations. In 2021, he participated as postdoctoral researcher in the TZUKU project from 2021 to 2022 for research in computing methods for the analysis and classification of behavioral contexts in rescue and assistance dogs.

From 2022 to 2025 he is a Postdoctoral Researcher with the Faculty of Science of the Autonomous University of San Luis Potosi (UASLP), conducting research on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning methods for 6G Communication Systems aided by Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS).
